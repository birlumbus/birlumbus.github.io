About these comparisons and sources

These globes use 4096 × 2048 numerical height atlases sampled from the actual saved surfaces. They are overviews; the rendered spheres are not displaced native meshes. The examples document different approaches, rather than a controlled ranking. Terrain colors share a scale; each generated world retains its saved sea level. The shading multiplier changes slope lighting only. Heights and shorelines remain the saved data. Nearest native-vertex sampling is used for generated worlds; Earth is bilinear from the complete ETOPO grid. Plate colors identify ownership within a world and do not correspond across worlds.

Earth terrain comes from NOAA ETOPO 2022. Its plate view uses Peter Bird’s PB2002 boundary data; no geological material field is inferred from elevation. Continental material is the saved categorical crust field, which may extend underwater. Its display uses nearest saved physical samples and does not add fine geological resolution. Land and sea is the above-water surface.
